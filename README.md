# 📊 Amazon Stock Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The Amazon Stock Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics and visualization project focused on analyzing Amazon's historical stock market performance from 1997 to 2025.

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations to understand stock price movements, trading volume, monthly performance, and yearly trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze Amazon's historical stock market data.
- Track opening, closing, highest, and lowest stock prices.
- Analyze trading volume patterns.
- Compare stock performance across different weekdays.
- Identify monthly and yearly stock price trends.
- Present meaningful insights through interactive visualizations.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

1. **KPI Cards:** Average High, Low, Open, and Close prices.
2. **Volume Over Time:** Trading volume distribution across weekdays.
3. **Open vs Close by Day:** Comparison of average opening and closing prices.
4. **Trading Volume Analysis:** Monthly trading volume comparison.
5. **Monthly Average Adjusted Close Price:** Analysis of adjusted closing prices across all months.
6. **High vs Low by Day:** Comparison of average daily high and low prices.
7. **Yearly Trends in Adjusted Close:** Historical adjusted closing price trends from 1997 to 2025.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters for:
- Weekdays (Monday–Friday)
- Years (1997–2025)
- Months (January–December)

These filters help users explore stock market performance across different time periods.
